Understand what happens before, during, and after a call.

01 Off by default02 During your call03 Your choices04 On your behalf05 Our boundaries
01

Off by default

That includes call audio, transcripts, call history, and extra profile data — off by default. Nothing is saved after a call unless you turn saving on.

02

While a call is happening

While a call is happening, audio passes through our providers so the call can run (Telnyx; OpenAI). When you haven’t opted in, we don’t keep it.

03

If you opt in

If you opt in, you choose what to keep (audio, transcript, and/or history). You can turn that off and ask us to delete what was saved.

04

On the call

On the call: the other party hears that it’s on behalf of your first name. Treena doesn’t say the product name.

05

We don’t sell your content as ads

We don’t sell your content as ads. You choose what to save, and you can ask us to delete what you’ve saved.
